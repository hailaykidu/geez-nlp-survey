\begin{abstract}
This survey systematically reviews 70 peer-reviewed papers (2016--2026) and discovers 16 foundational NLP tools for Ge'ez-script languages (Amharic, Tigrinya, Classical Ge'ez). Organized across four technical pillars, our analysis reveals: (a)~critical underrepresentation of Classical Ge'ez (2 tools, 12\%); (b)~severe P4 bottleneck (sequence labeling: 1 tool, 6\%); (c)~71\% single-language tools; (d)~65\% rule-based approaches. We propose a five-priority research agenda for inclusive, script-aware NLP development.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Ge'ez script, low-resource NLP, Amharic, Tigrinya, foundational tools, systematic review

\section{Introduction}
Natural language processing advances have accrued unevenly. Ge'ez-script languages (62M+ speakers) remain critically underrepresented despite linguistic complexity and digital significance. This survey fills a critical gap by conducting systematic, tool-centered analysis. We analyze 70 papers and systematically discover 16 foundational tools organized across four technical pillars: tokenization, morphology, embeddings, and sequence labeling.

Key contributions: (1)~First tool-centered systematic review for Ge'ez-script NLP; (2)~Script-level linguistic perspective; (3)~Quantified disparities; (4)~Five-priority research agenda.

\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Literature Review}
Systematic search (PRISMA) across ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, EACL, AACL, COLING, LREC (2016--2026). Inclusion: peer-reviewed publications on Ge'ez-script NLP. Exclusion: preprints, non-peer-reviewed, downstream applications without foundational contribution. Result: 70 papers.

\subsection{Tool Discovery}
Parallel discovery across five repositories (GitHub, Zenodo, PyPI, HuggingFace, DBLP). Extracted 30 metadata fields per tool. Deduplicated (semantic similarity 0.75), manually verified. Scope: Ge'ez-script languages ONLY. Result: 16 tools.

\section{Discussion}

Key findings: (1)~Language disparity: Ge'ez critically underrepresented (12\%); (2)~P4 bottleneck: 1 tool prevents end-to-end systems; (3)~Design: 71\% single-language, 65\% rule-based; (4)~Growth: ~1→2.3 tools/year indicates maturation; (5)~Cross-lingual transfer unexplored (only 31\% multilingual).

\section{Five-Priority Research Agenda}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Unified script-aware tokenization for all Ge'ez-script languages
    \item Cross-lingual morphological transfer learning
    \item Script-optimized embeddings and language models
    \item Expanded sequence labeling benchmarks and tools
    \item Centralized community infrastructure and governance
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

This systematic review of 70 papers and 16 tools reveals a fragmented landscape with sharp disparities in language and pillar coverage. Over 62 million speakers rely on Ge'ez-script languages, yet tool development lags far behind. This survey provides a foundation for coordinated community advancement toward inclusive, script-aware NLP.
